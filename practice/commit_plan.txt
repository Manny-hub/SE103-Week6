Why smaller commits are better than one giant commit:
Smaller commits make changes easier to understand, review, and debug. They help identify errors quickly and make it easier to undo a specific change without affecting other work. Each commit documents a specific improvement, making the project history clearer and easier to maintain. This approach also encourages careful testing before changes are committed.
